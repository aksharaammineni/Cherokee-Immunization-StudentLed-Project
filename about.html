About This Initiative

This community awareness initiative was created to address declining kindergarten immunization rates in Cherokee County, Georgia.

With kindergarten vaccination coverage reported at 83.81%, there is an important opportunity to improve awareness about childhood immunizations and help families understand the requirements for school entry.

Through research, conversations with parents, and the distribution of educational resources, this project aims to:

  • Increase awareness of local kindergarten immunization statistics.
  • Educate parents about Georgia’s school-entry vaccination requirements.
  • Understand the questions and challenges families experience.
  • Connect parents with reliable public-health resources.
  • Encourage informed conversations between families and healthcare professionals.
