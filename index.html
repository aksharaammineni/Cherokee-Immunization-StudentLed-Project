Why this matters now

Kindergarten is an important milestone in every child’s life. As children prepare to enter school, ensuring that their immunizations are up to date helps protect them, their classmates, teachers, and the wider community from preventable diseases.

However, Cherokee County faces a concerning decline in kindergarten immunization coverage. By raising awareness, sharing reliable information, and listening to parents, this initiative aims to help close the immunization gap and promote healthier school communities.

Small steps can make a significant difference. Start by reviewing your child’s immunization records and learning about Georgia’s school-entry requirements.
