Listening to Cherokee County Parents

Behind every public-health statistic are real families making decisions about their children’s health. Conversations with local parents help explore how families understand kindergarten immunization requirements, where they obtain vaccine information, and what challenges they experience. The goal is to understand parental perspectives and identify ways to improve awareness, communication, and access to reliable information.
