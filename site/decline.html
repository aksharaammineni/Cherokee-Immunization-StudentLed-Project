Understanding the Factors Behind the Decline

There is no single explanation for declining kindergarten immunization rates. Multiple factors can influence whether children receive their recommended vaccinations and whether their records are properly documented.

Vaccine exemptions

Cherokee County’s reported religious exemption rate is 12.67%. Religious exemptions can contribute to lower overall vaccination coverage and increase the number of children who are susceptible to preventable diseases.

Vaccine hesitancy and misinformation

Parents may encounter conflicting information about vaccine safety, effectiveness, and potential side effects. Online misinformation can make it difficult to distinguish reliable medical information from inaccurate claims.

Missed booster doses

Some children receive their early childhood vaccinations but miss important doses needed before entering kindergarten. Families may not realize that additional doses are due around ages four to six.

Administrative and documentation gaps

Some children may have received their vaccinations but lack updated records. Missing documentation, delayed transfers, and incomplete immunization histories can affect reported school-entry coverage.

A message to parents

Every family has questions, and those questions deserve respectful answers. The purpose of this initiative is not to judge parents, but to make accurate information more accessible and encourage conversations with trusted healthcare professionals.

Common misconceptionWhat parents should know
