What parents can do

  1. 1Locate their child’s existing immunization records.
  2. 2Schedule a review with their pediatrician or local health department.
  3. 3Ask whether any additional doses are needed.
  4. 4Obtain an updated Georgia Form 3231.
  5. 5Keep a copy of their child’s immunization documentation.

Common questions

▸Where can I get Form 3231?

A Georgia-licensed healthcare provider or local health department can issue the certificate.

▸What if my child received vaccines in another state?

Bring the existing immunization records to a Georgia healthcare provider or health department so the information can be reviewed and transferred appropriately.

▸What if my child has a medical exemption?

Medical exemptions must be documented according to Georgia requirements on the appropriate immunization certificate.

▸Does Georgia allow religious exemptions?

Yes. Georgia recognizes religious objections through the appropriate Affidavit of Religious Objection to Immunization, Form 2208. Parents should consult Georgia DPH for current instructions.
